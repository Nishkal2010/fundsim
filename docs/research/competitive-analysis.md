# Competitive Analysis

## Summary: FundSim's Position

FundSim is the only free, browser-native deal simulator covering PE, VC, and IB in a single product — no Excel required, no paywall, no install. Every competitor either charges $290–$1,500/year, requires Excel/desktop software, or focuses on video content with downloadable templates rather than live interactive computation. FundSim's closest threat is FinanceQuest (also free, browser-based), but FinanceQuest targets early-stage learners with gamified lessons rather than institutional-depth deal mechanics.

---

## Competitors

### Wall Street Prep

- **URL**: wallstreetprep.com
- **Price**: $499 one-time (Premium Package, 7 courses); bootcamps from $1,699; Wharton joint certificate $4,800
- **What they offer**: 45+ hours of video instruction covering financial statement modeling, DCF, M&A, comps (trading + transaction), and LBO. Aimed at students and analysts preparing for IB recruiting. Institutional credibility is high — used by bulge-bracket banks for internal training. Also offers live tutoring at $800/hour and PitchBook access (1 month).
- **Interactive models**: No — content is video + downloadable Excel templates. Students watch and replicate; there is no browser-based engine that reacts to their inputs in real time.
- **FundSim advantage**: Free vs. $499+. Browser-based with live computation vs. passive video + Excel download. FundSim lets a student see a waterfall shift as they change carried interest from 20% to 25% in real time; WSP requires them to build that in Excel themselves after watching a lecture.

---

### Breaking Into Wall Street (BIWS)

- **URL**: breakingintowallstreet.com
- **Price**: $497/year (Platinum annual); $1,497 lifetime one-time. Individual courses from $97.
- **What they offer**: 1,434 videos, 130 downloadable Excel models, 57 written guides (1,500+ pages). Covers IB modeling, PE/LBO, M&A, DCF, accounting, and interview prep. The largest pure-volume library in the space — strong on case studies and technical interview prep for 56,000+ members.
- **Interactive models**: No — all content is video + Excel files. No browser-based simulator. Learning is passive watching + local Excel work.
- **FundSim advantage**: Free vs. $497/year. No download or Excel required. FundSim's deal score rubric and shareable links have no equivalent in BIWS. BIWS wins on breadth of interview prep and volume; FundSim wins on zero-friction, zero-cost deal simulation with instant feedback.

---

### Macabacus

- **URL**: macabacus.com
- **Price**: $200/year (Basic), $360/year (Professional), Enterprise custom
- **What they offer**: Excel and PowerPoint productivity add-in for finance professionals — 100+ shortcuts, formula auditing, model-checking, chart templates, and Excel-to-PowerPoint linking. Not an educational product; it is a professional tool for analysts already working at banks, PE firms, and consulting firms. Target user is a working analyst, not a student.
- **Interactive models**: No — it enhances Excel, it is not a simulator. There are no deal simulations, no PE/VC mechanics, no learning content.
- **FundSim advantage**: Different category entirely. FundSim teaches the mechanics; Macabacus speeds up the workflow of people who already know them. No head-to-head competition, but students sometimes confuse "finance tools" as a category. FundSim is free and requires nothing installed.

---

### Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)

- **URL**: corporatefinanceinstitute.com
- **Price**: ~$290–$350/year (individual); 50% student discount available; limited free previews per course
- **What they offer**: 250+ courses across certifications like FMVA (Financial Modeling & Valuation Analyst), CBCA, CMSA, BIDA. Broad curriculum covering Excel modeling, accounting, valuation, FP&A, credit analysis, and capital markets. Strong on certification credentials for career advancement. More of an online university for finance than a deal simulator.
- **Interactive models**: Partial — some courses include browser-based exercises, but the core delivery is video + downloadable Excel templates. No real-time deal simulation engine.
- **FundSim advantage**: Free vs. $290–$350/year for access to deal-relevant content. CFI's breadth is an advantage for certification-seekers, but for a student who wants to run a PE waterfall or build a cap table tonight without paying or downloading anything, FundSim is the only option. CFI wins on certification credentials; FundSim wins on instant, zero-cost hands-on simulation.

---

### FinanceQuest

- **URL**: financequest.app
- **Price**: Free (core content and sandbox); premium tiers likely for advanced features
- **What they offer**: Gamified finance learning platform with 300+ lessons, 30 learning tracks (DCF, LBO, comps, M&A, accounting), a browser-based modeling sandbox, and an AI interview simulator with questions from Goldman, Blackstone, Morgan Stanley, and JP Morgan. Closest to FundSim in format — browser-native, no Excel required.
- **Interactive models**: Yes — browser-based sandbox that mirrors Excel-style financial modeling; AI interview lab. However, emphasis is on learning/progression through gamified tracks rather than running a full PE fund or building a live cap table with real institutional mechanics (waterfall tiers, SAFE conversions, J-curve, PME).
- **FundSim advantage**: FundSim goes deeper on the institutional mechanics of PE (European/American waterfalls, GP/LP economics, PME vs. S&P 500, DSCR/covenants), VC (SAFE note conversion, anti-dilution, power law portfolio construction), and IB (100-point deal score, full football field). FinanceQuest is better for early-stage learners building foundational knowledge; FundSim is better for students who want to simulate an actual deal with institutional-depth parameters. FundSim also has shareable deal links, 8 preset deals, and a DECA prep mode — features FinanceQuest lacks.

---

### AmplifyME

- **URL**: amplifyme.com
- **Price**: Free (2-hour live simulation events); paid bootcamps available
- **What they offer**: Live, scheduled simulation events (Markets, Banking/M&A, Quant) that run 2 hours each, replicating real role-play scenarios used by top financial institutions. Events are free; students sign up and attend at a scheduled time. No PE or VC content. UK-centric (London bootcamp offered).
- **Interactive models**: Yes, but event-based (not self-paced). No persistent simulator a student can return to.
- **FundSim advantage**: FundSim is available 24/7 with no scheduling required. It covers PE and VC, which AmplifyME does not. FundSim's deal state persists (saved to Supabase) and is shareable — AmplifyME sessions are ephemeral. AmplifyME wins on live cohort experience; FundSim wins on depth, persistence, and asynchronous access.
