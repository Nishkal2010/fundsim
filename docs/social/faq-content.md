# FAQ — fundsimulate.com

---

**1. What is FundSim?**
FundSim is a free deal simulator for finance students. It lets you build LBO models, VC cap tables, and IB deal analyses in your browser — no spreadsheet setup, no prior experience required. Think of it as a flight simulator, but for private equity and venture capital deals.

---

**2. Is it really free?**
Yes, completely. There's no paywall, no free trial that expires, and no "Pro" tier hiding the useful features. Everything on the site is free to use.

---

**3. Do I need finance experience to use it?**
No. FundSim is built for students who are still learning. Each simulator walks you through the inputs step by step, and FinFox (the built-in AI assistant) can explain any concept you're unsure about. If you know what a company's revenue is and want to understand what a buyout might look like, you can use FundSim today.

---

**4. What simulators are included?**
FundSim currently includes three core modules: a PE/LBO simulator (entry price, debt structure, exit multiple, IRR/MOIC output), a VC cap table tool (dilution modeling from Seed through Series B, SAFEs and priced rounds), and an IB deal mechanics module covering M&A accretion/dilution and synergy analysis. More are in development.

---

**5. What is FinFox?**
FinFox is FundSim's AI deal assistant, built on Claude. You can ask it to explain concepts ("what's a PIK toggle?"), walk through your model's assumptions, or help you think through a deal scenario. It's scoped to finance and deal work — it won't write your essay or plan your weekend.

---

**6. Can I use FundSim for DECA prep?**
Yes, and it's one of the primary use cases. DECA Finance events (Securities and Investments, Financial Services, etc.) require you to analyze deals and justify assumptions in real time. Practicing with live models — where you can change inputs and see the output shift — builds the fluency that makes a real difference in role-plays and written events.

---

**7. How accurate are the models?**
The underlying math is standard industry methodology — the same IRR, MOIC, and dilution calculations used in actual deal work. That said, FundSim is a learning tool, not a valuation platform. The models are accurate enough to build genuine intuition; they're not a substitute for a full financial model in a live deal process.

---

**8. Can I save my work?**
Yes. If you create a free account, your deal models are saved automatically. You can return to them later, tweak assumptions, and compare scenarios across sessions.

---

**9. Is there a mobile version?**
FundSim works in a mobile browser, though the full modeling experience is better on desktop. The core simulators are responsive — you can explore scenarios on your phone, but for serious prep work a laptop is recommended.

---

**10. How do I share my deal analysis?**
Each model has a share button that generates a link. Anyone with the link can view your deal structure and outputs without needing an account. It's useful for comparing models with classmates or showing a club officer what you built.

---

**11. What's next for FundSim?**
More deal types (real estate, infrastructure, growth equity), deeper IB modules, and scenario comparison tools are on the roadmap. The goal is to cover the full range of deals that come up in PE/VC/IB interviews and DECA events. If there's a specific model you want, the feedback button on the site goes directly to the founder.

---
