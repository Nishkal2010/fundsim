## Thread 3: "VC Cap Table Explained"

**Tweet 1**
You start a company. You own 100%.
Three funding rounds later, you own 25%.
Where did the other 75% go — and was it worth it? Here's how VC dilution actually works. 🧵 [167 chars]

**Tweet 2**
Seed round: you raise $500K on a SAFE at a $5M cap.
SAFEs don't dilute you immediately — they convert at the next priced round.
Founders still own 100% on paper. But the dilution is coming. [191 chars]

**Tweet 3**
Series A: $4M raised at $12M pre-money ($16M post).
The SAFE converts here. Investors get ~33% of the company combined (SAFE + new money).
Founders: 67%. You've just been diluted for the first time on a cap table. [215 chars]

**Tweet 4**
Series B: $15M raised at $45M pre-money ($60M post).
New investors take 25%. Existing holders — including founders — get diluted proportionally.
Founders end up at ~50% of 75% = ~38%. After options pool top-ups, closer to 25-30%. [232 chars]

**Tweet 5**
25% of a $100M company is $25M.
100% of nothing is $0.
Dilution isn't the enemy — bad terms, wrong investors, and low valuations are.
Model your own cap table and see how the math plays out: fundsimulate.com [210 chars]
