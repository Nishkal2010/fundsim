## The angle (why these creators, and what we're offering them)

FundSim is a free, no-signup web app where finance students run live PE / VC / IB deals (LBO + waterfall, VC cap table + SAFE conversion, M&A deal scored out of 100) and **share a scorecard link** — so they can beat each other's scores on the same deal. The product's own homepage positions it directly against Wall Street Prep, Breaking Into Wall Street, and CFI: _"You've watched the videos. Now run the deal."_ It is the **practice layer** on top of the instruction these creators already sell or teach.

That's the pitch to a creator:

- Their audience **already wants reps** — they watch the technical-question videos, then have nowhere free to apply them.
- FundSim is **free, instant, no account** — zero friction for a creator to point a video at.
- The **shareable scorecard** is the content hook: "post your deal score, tag a friend, beat it." A creator can run a challenge against their own audience (their score vs. their followers' scores) — that _is_ the video.
- It does **not compete** with a creator's paid course — it's the free sandbox that makes their paid teaching land. Frame it as complementary, never as a replacement.

Realistic ask, in order of how much it costs the creator:

1. **Try it and tell me what's wrong** (lowest friction, highest reply rate — leads with humility, not a favor).
2. **Mention / link it** if it's actually good.
3. **Run a "beat my score" challenge** with their audience (the real prize).

Do **not** lead with "feature my product." Lead with "you teach the theory; I built the free place to practice it — would your audience use it?"

## Sending notes for the founder

- **Personalize the bracketed line — do not skip it.** Watch one recent video / scroll one recent post and reference something specific and true. This single line is the difference between a reply and a delete.
- **Order to send:** Tier A first (1–6), especially Afzal (clean email) and Peak Frameworks (media kit invites collaboration). Tier B and any remaining **[verify]** creators after you've confirmed handles and contact.
- **For BIWS / M&I and Wall Street Mastermind specifically:** lead even harder on "free, complementary, nothing to sell your audience." They sell paid courses/mentorship — you want to read as the free sandbox their audience graduates into, never a competitor.
- **One follow-up max,** ~7–10 days later, two lines: "Circling back in case this got buried — no pressure either way." Then stop.
- **Track replies** so a warm creator gets Template C within a day, while interest is fresh.
- **Do not buy placements or DM-blast.** This is a small, hand-personalized batch; that's the whole point.
