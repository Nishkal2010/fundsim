# FundSim Persona Roundtable — Debrief

_5 simulated users with distinct lives debated FundSim across 3 rounds (first reactions → "is there a market" → "the one change that makes me a champion"), then a neutral analyst synthesized it. Run date 2026-06-06._

## The cast

| Persona                    | Life                                                                           | Lens                                           |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Marcus Chen, 26**        | Ex-Goldman IB analyst, now first-year PE associate at a $3B MM fund            | Realism / does the math survive scrutiny       |
| **Priya Nair, 19**         | UT Austin sophomore, VP of Education of the investment club, gunning for IB SA | Recruiting edge / distribution to 200 members  |
| **Dr. Eleanor Brandt, 54** | Clinical finance professor, teaches Corp Fin + PE/VC elective                  | Pedagogy / assessment / curriculum fit         |
| **Diego Salazar, 31**      | Ex-mechanical engineer, career-switcher in a part-time MSF at a non-target     | Accessibility / onboarding / the outsider      |
| **Tasha Williams, 34**     | Ex-edtech founder, now seed VC                                                 | TAM / retention / moat / vitamin-vs-painkiller |

## What they liked (consensus)

1. **The shareable live-deal link** — the single most-praised feature. Marcus, Priya, Diego, Tasha all flagged it independently as the real differentiator vs. static Excel/PDF, and the only feature with genuine network-effect potential.
2. **The PE waterfall with European vs. American / ILPA clawback** — read as credible "real infrastructure" to the two most technical people (Marcus, Eleanor). It's what made the skeptics give it more than 30 seconds.
3. **No-account, no-install entry** — all five endorsed it. Tasha: a deliberate decision that dodges the signup wall where "80% of consumer ed products die."
4. **Drag-and-watch-it-recompute** — Eleanor called it a teaching instrument she "cannot manufacture in a 75-minute class."
5. **FinFox's potential** — but only conditionally (see dislikes).

## What they disliked (consensus)

1. **Onboarding — "where do I start"** is the repeated killer. Diego: _"the fog hits at second zero and I close the tab."_ All five converged on it as the leak that kills both markets before the product is even evaluated.
2. **Solo high-school-founder uptime risk** — peak fall recruiting season collides with college-essay season. Tasha raised it; Eleanor and Marcus conceded it's a structural precondition, not a quibble.
3. **Split positioning** — DECA Prep Mode sitting next to European-waterfall mechanics confuses whether this is a serious deal tool or a high-school competition app.
4. **Serves two non-overlapping markets with no path between them** — Tasha's "aspirin, not painkiller": tries to serve both the recruiting junior and the career-switcher, locks in neither.
5. **FinFox could be merely definitional** — "1.3x is above the floor" teaches nothing. Everyone who valued it conditioned that on it being _interrogative_ ("yes, but watch what happens if revenue drops 15%").

## Where they actually fought

- **Marcus vs. Priya — DECA Mode:** positioning poison (Marcus) vs. the only mechanic that gets 200 freshmen to show up (Priya).
- **Tasha vs. Priya — the high-school-founder framing:** built-in expiration date on founder motivation (Tasha) vs. incentive that's perfectly aligned toward real users _right now_ (Priya).
- **Tasha vs. Diego/Eleanor — "two markets":** fatal scope creep → reframed as a _sequencing_ problem because "Diego eventually becomes Priya" and the math is identical; only the first 90 seconds differ. Tasha partially conceded.
- **Marcus vs. the room's enthusiasm — the shareable link:** Marcus showed it's a double-edged grenade — a broken model sent to a banker makes the candidate look _worse_. Priya fully conceded: _"The link is only a weapon if you know how to use it. Otherwise it's a grenade you hand to a freshman."_

## Market verdict

Real but **narrow**, and a painkiller only for a thin, time-boxed slice: the recruiting-track junior/MBA in the ~6-week window before first-round technicals. That segment is intense but **spiky** — they use it hard, get the offer, never return. That's an **acquisition channel, not a retained market.**

The only **recurring** engagement comes from two places, both currently blocked:

- **Eleanor's segment (professors)** — scalable and recurring (240 students/yr from _one_ departmental conversation), but gated on an instructor "assignment mode" + a credible uptime commitment.
- **Diego's segment (career-switchers)** — larger, loyal, returns repeatedly — but only if onboarding holds their hand.

**Net:** today it behaves more like a vitamin because nobody can self-locate in the first 90 seconds. It becomes a painkiller the moment onboarding routes each user to their reason-to-stay.

## Who actually engages & evangelizes (monetization deferred, so read as "shows up + spreads it")

- **Priya = highest-leverage distribution node.** A Club Challenge/leaderboard mode → she demos to 200 members _this week_ and spreads it across the Texas Finance Club listserv (8+ schools).
- **Eleanor = only recurring, scalable adopter.** 240 students/yr — but conditional on assignment mode + public uptime.
- **Diego = large underserved loyal base** — returns repeatedly _if_ onboarding works.
- **Marcus = niche but credibility-minting** (~20 analysts) — only with a stress-test mode + bulletproof math. His endorsement is what makes the shareable link credible to interviewers.
- The recruiting-junior end-user **does not retain** — a 6-week spike.

## Ranked improvements (impact × effort)

| #   | Change                                                                                                                                                       | Effort | Impact | Whose unlock                                            |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------ | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |

| 3   | **Instructor "Assignment Mode"**: lock params + require a short written assumption-defense before the link generates                                         | medium | high   | Eleanor (the only recurring/future-revenue path)        |
| 4   | **Club Challenge / leaderboard mode**: VP sets a scenario, locks inputs, 48-hr leaderboard by IRR/MOIC/deal score, shareable results                         | medium | high   | Priya (highest-velocity distribution)                   |
| 5   | **Make FinFox Socratic, not definitional** — answer questions with a stress-challenge                                                                        | medium | high   | everyone who valued FinFox                              |
| 6   | **One-click stress-test toggle** on the PE waterfall (rev −15%, hold +2yrs, exit −1 turn) + FinFox names which input killed IRR                              | medium | medium | Marcus                                                  |
| 7   | **Subordinate/route DECA Mode** so a recruiting junior never sees it on the default first screen (gate it behind the Start-Here goal question)               | low    | medium | Marcus, Eleanor                                         |

## The one line to tape to the wall

> The math is real and the shareable link is a genuine wedge — but the product lives or dies in the first 90 seconds. Fix onboarding and make one credible trust+adoption commitment (instructor mode + a public status page), or the painkiller never gets found.
