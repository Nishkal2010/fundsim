That's the gap we built FundSim to close.

---

## The Problem with Passive Finance Education

Finance education is almost entirely passive. You read about leveraged buyouts in a textbook. You watch a professor work through a DCF on a whiteboard. You memorize the formula for IRR. None of it requires you to make a decision, take a risk, or feel the consequence of a wrong assumption.

Then you sit across from a partner who's done fifty deals and expects you to think like someone who's done five. The gap between what you studied and what they're testing for is enormous — and no amount of re-reading fills it.

The students who show up ready have usually built models on their own, made mistakes, and figured out why they were wrong. They've felt the feedback loop. That's not a textbook skill — it's a practice skill.

---

## What FundSim Does Differently

**1. Every concept is immediately interactive.**

In FundSim, you don't read about debt/equity splits — you set them. Drag the leverage ratio from 50% to 70% and watch IRR climb. Push it to 85% and watch the model flag insufficient debt service coverage. The feedback is instant and visceral. You learn through doing, not through remembering.

**2. FinFox is your AI deal tutor.**

FinFox is our built-in AI tutor. Ask it why your waterfall isn't distributing correctly. Ask it to explain the difference between a GP catch-up and a hurdle rate. Ask it what happens to equity value when EBITDA multiple contracts at exit. It answers in plain English, in context, without you having to leave the model. It's the difference between having a senior associate sitting next to you and staring at a formula you don't understand.

**3. Realistic deal scenarios, not toy problems.**

FundSim ships with real-feeling scenarios: a take-private of a $400M consumer brand, a Series B cap table negotiation, an IB pitch for a strategic acquisition. These aren't simplified textbook problems. They have messy numbers, competing stakeholders, and trade-offs with no clean answer — the same shape as the cases you'll see in interviews.
