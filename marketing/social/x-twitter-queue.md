"what's a soft hurdle vs a hard hurdle"

soft: you clear the hurdle, then GP gets carry on *all* profits (catch-up makes them whole)
hard: GP only gets carry on profits *above* the hurdle

99% of modern funds use soft.

see it move: fundsimulate.com/#pe
