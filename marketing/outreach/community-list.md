## Hard rules across all channels
1. Always be a person, never a brand voice.
2. Always lead with "free, no signup, no paywall" — it's the lede.
3. Always answer the question "why are you doing this for free" honestly.
4. Always reply to every comment for the first 4 hours after posting.
5. Never copy-paste the same text into two channels back to back.
